What it does

A fashion editor in your pocket

Every morning, Fashion Bot picks a style challenge inspired by a different decade, film world, or cultural moment — and builds a full outfit around it.

Weather-aware outfits

Your outfit suggestion accounts for the day's forecast — no more showing up underdressed in the rain.

🎨

Curated colour palettes

Each look comes with a three-colour story shown as swatches — from earthy saturates to jewel tones to muted brights.

📅

Calendar-connected

Connect Google Calendar so your outfit fits your day — big meeting, casual Friday, or night out sorted.

✦

Two looks, every day

A primary look and an alternative, each with specific items, fabrics, and a mood line to set the scene.

🕐

Tomorrow, sorted tonight

Fashion Bot thinks ahead — your tomorrow outfit is ready when you wake up, with a daily reminder at a time you choose.

📖

Outfit history

Browse the last two weeks of outfit challenges and export your favourites to save or share.

How it works

Three steps to your best dressed day

01

Set your style profile

Tell Fashion Bot your city, style keywords, work days, and any personal style notes — items you avoid, colours you love, your vibe.

02

Get your daily challenge

Every day, AI generates a completely different style challenge — a new world, a new look, a new colour story — tailored to your day and weather.

03

Get dressed with confidence

Two full outfits, specific items, styling tips, and a Pinterest mood board. Add the look to your calendar and go.
