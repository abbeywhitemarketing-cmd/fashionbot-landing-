Common questions

FAQ

How does Fashion Bot generate my outfits?

Fashion Bot uses AI to create a daily outfit recommendation tailored to your style profile, the weather in your city, and the events on your calendar. Every day brings a different style challenge — a new world, a new look, a new colour story.

How do I connect my Google Calendar?

Go to Settings in the app and tap "Connect Google Calendar". You'll be taken through a secure sign-in flow. Once connected, Fashion Bot will read your upcoming events to tailor your outfit to your day.

How do I change my style preferences?

Tap the settings icon in the top right of the home screen. From there you can update your city, style keywords, work days, and any special instructions.

Can I regenerate my outfit?

Yes — pull down to refresh on the home screen to generate a new outfit for that day.

How do I turn off daily notifications?

Go to Settings and toggle off "Daily reminder". You can also change the reminder time from the same screen.

How do I view past outfits?

Tap "See outfit history" at the bottom of the home screen to browse the last 14 days of outfit challenges. You can export any outfit using the share button.
